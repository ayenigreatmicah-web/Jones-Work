Featured Projects

Each project represents another opportunity to practice design, composition, and creativity.

Marcus Rashford Design

The Marcus Rashford design was created as a creative exercise in combining photography, typography, and a strong visual layout. The aim was to make the subject stand out while keeping the overall design clean and easy to follow.

Working on this design helped me experiment with balance, image placement, contrast, and the way a viewer moves through a composition.

Manchester United Design

The Manchester United design explores how a strong club identity can be translated into a simple digital composition. I focused on creating a layout where the image and title work together without making the page feel crowded.

This project gave me another opportunity to practice visual hierarchy and composition while keeping the design bold and balanced.

Cristiano Ronaldo Wallpaper

The Cristiano Ronaldo wallpaper design focuses on creating a strong focal point around the player while keeping the composition suitable for a digital wallpaper.

Creating different versions of the design helped me understand how images, spacing, and text can change the mood of a page.
