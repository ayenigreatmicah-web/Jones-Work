Blogs

Welcome to the Jones Work blog section. Here I share selected designs, experiments, and projects that reflect my progress as I learn and build. Each piece represents a chance to practice, explore an idea, and create something that feels different from the last.

Manchester United Design

The Manchester United design explores how a strong club identity can be translated into a simple digital composition. I focused on creating a layout where the image and title work together without making the page feel crowded. This project gave me another opportunity to practice visual hierarchy and composition. I wanted the final piece to feel bold while still keeping enough breathing room around the main subject and supporting elements.

Cristiano Ronaldo Manchester United Wallpaper Design

The Cristiano Ronaldo wallpaper design focuses on creating a strong focal point around the player while keeping the composition suitable for a digital wallpaper. It combines a bold image with a simple presentation to keep attention on the main subject. Creating different versions of the design helped me understand how images, spacing, and text can change the mood of a page. Projects like this are part of my ongoing practice as I continue developing my design and front-end skills.
