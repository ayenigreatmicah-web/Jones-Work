My Journey

Jones Work is a growing portfolio built around my journey into technology. I enjoy exploring web development, creating clean layouts, working with HTML and CSS, and finding better ways to turn simple ideas into functional digital experiences.

Every project gives me something new to improve — from responsive design and page structure to typography, spacing, and user experience. I am focused on building practical skills that can grow into bigger and more meaningful projects.
